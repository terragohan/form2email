{% extends "guide-base.html" %} {% block heading %}A feedback form that emails you{% endblock %} {% block standfirst %}A quick 1–5 rating plus a comment box, linked from your thank-you page or post-purchase email — every response delivered to your inbox.{% endblock %} {% block about %}

Right after a purchase or a delivered order is when customers are most willing to tell you how it went. A small feedback form — one rating, one comment box — catches that moment, and the responses pile up somewhere you actually read: your inbox.

If your store or thank-you page is static HTML, it can't receive the form POST itself. Form2Email receives it and emails you the rating and comment. There is no server code to write and no account to create.

That's the whole integration. The form works with or without JavaScript — swap the radio buttons for a star widget if you like, as long as the chosen value is submitted as rating. Only the action, method="post", and the input name attributes matter to Form2Email.

{% endblock %} {% block mapping %}

Each submission becomes one email to your destination address:

  • message (the comments) appears in the body, along with name when the visitor gives one.
  • Reply-To is the visitor's email — but only when they fill it in, so you can answer the people who asked for a reply.
  • rating arrives listed as an additional field, like any other extra field you add (an order number, a product name, anything).

With _redirect set, the visitor's browser is sent to your thank-you page after a successful submit. Without it they get a small JSON response — fine for JavaScript-driven forms, less friendly for plain HTML.

{% endblock %} {% block tips %}
  • Ask for the rating first. It's one click, so nearly everyone answers it — lead with the comment box and response rates drop.
  • Keep optional fields optional. A feedback form that demands a name and email gets abandoned; mark them optional and say why email is there.
  • Reply-To works when the visitor gives an email — label it "only if you'd like a reply" so people understand the trade.
  • Make the button say what happens: "Send feedback", not "Submit".
  • Add {{ settings.mail_from }} to your contacts so the verification and submission emails don't land in spam.
{% endblock %} {% block limitations %}
  • A feedback form is always-on, so remember the endpoint expires {{ settings.default_ttl_days }} days after verification — extend it for free from your manage page (extending also revives an expired endpoint), or ask the operator for a permanent endpoint.
  • Submissions are accepted only from the exact domain you registered. If your site answers on both yoursite.com and www.yoursite.com, register the one your form is served from (or create one endpoint per hostname).
  • Visitors get no confirmation email — Form2Email only ever emails you. Say your thanks on the _redirect page instead.
  • rating counts toward the extra-field limits: up to 20 additional fields of 1,000 characters each, comments up to 20,000 characters.
