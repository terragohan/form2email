Set it up

  1. Create the endpoint

    Use the tool on the homepage — or POST {{ settings.base_url.rstrip('/') }}/api/endpoints — with the email that receives submissions and the exact domain this form lives on.

  2. Verify the email

    We send a verification link to the destination address — single-use, valid 24 hours. Nothing is accepted until a human clicks it. Add {{ settings.mail_from }} to your trusted senders.

  3. Point the form at it

    Put your public URL in the form's action. Submissions are only accepted from the domain you registered — exact hostname match, subdomains need their own endpoint.

  4. Keep it alive

    Endpoints stay active for {{ settings.default_ttl_days }} days after verification. Extend for free (+{{ settings.default_ttl_days }} days, as often as you like) from your manage page — extending also revives an expired endpoint.
